Privacy and responsible participation

We request account information to operate the platform and associate survey responses with member accounts. Do not submit passwords, identification numbers or unnecessary sensitive medical information in survey answers.

Survey participation should be voluntary. Read each questionnaire before answering. If a survey requests sensitive health information, its purpose and handling should be explained before participation.
